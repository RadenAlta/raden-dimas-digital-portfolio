Static Website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no backend or database. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or its own cookies.

Contact form

The form validates data in the browser and prepares a mailto link. The site does not send messages automatically or store form details. Draft handling follows your email provider after the email app opens.

Theme Preferences

The dark or light theme preference is stored in localStorage with the key raden-portfolio-theme. This value stores only a display preference, not form content or identity.

External Links

The site provides links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and Google Accredible. Each service policy applies after a link is opened. The site does not send form data to those services.

Experience and Media

Certificate images are published with permission. Visible numbers are not copied into additional metadata. Transcripts and graduation letters are not published. The decorative Home video is cleared for public use and contains no audio or personal data.

Corrections and Deletion

Requests to correct or remove website information can be sent to radendimas.work@gmail.com.

Policy Changes

This page may be updated when site features change. The revision date appears above and important changes will be reflected here.

Questions about Privacy?

Contact me through the available public channels if information needs correction or updating.
